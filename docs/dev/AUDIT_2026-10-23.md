## Bugs the audit caught (selected)

- **Physics that couldn't work:**
  - A Dynamo could power its own Motor forever (free energy). Fixed with a braking torque and 60 % efficiency.
  - The Turbine produced no torque from axial wind. Fixed with a 25° blade-pitch tangential force.
  - A real balloon lifts grams, so "Helio lifts a bucket" was impossible. Fixed with toy-helium lift of about 0.5 kg.
  - A 2 N·m Motor peaks at about 4.7 W, which contradicted its 120 W rating. Fixed with a 30 N·m stall torque (about 71 W at the shaft).
  - A hamster-driven Dynamo gave 0.9 W, too little for any Lamp. Fixed with toy-strength critter torques.
  - FULCRUM's ±30° range is geometrically impossible at its size. It's ±12° now.
- **Fit failures:**
  - The robot vacuum didn't fit the Trap Box. Box is now 7×5×5 cells.
  - Big balls didn't fit the Rift Gates. Gates are now 4×4×0.5 cells.
  - Bandit couldn't reach the far pedal.
  - Pip's wheel socket sat outside the wheel.
  - The Big Top's barrel left its own bounds.
  - Waterwheel collision boxes couldn't hold water.
  - Plugs seated deeper than their sockets and covered the LED rings.
- **Gameplay chains that couldn't fire:**
  - The quick-start machine's falling domino couldn't set off dynamite.
  - Recipe 3 never switched the Torch on.
  - Recipe 4 needed 40 W from a hamster.
  - A 1.5 N·s Jitterjuice trigger meant the Cork Blaster could never set it off.
- **Spec drift:**
  - Mismatched sizes for about 12 catalogue rows.
  - Look-dev versus catalogue scale for the Motor, Gale, Mains and Bangstick.
  - Tier names (GB vs Low/Ultra).
  - Key conflicts: Enter for chat vs keyboard building, and F11 vs browser fullscreen.
  - Thread-count rule.
  - Light caps.
  - A ±33,554 km position range that is really ±3,355 km.

## Open for design review (values editors had to choose)

None of these block development; they are tuning values.

1. Cone half-angles, Vac hold limit (0.5 kg), Inflator timing, Arc Tower strike rate, Burning Glass focal range, sound levels and critter dB thresholds, wind wake rule (SIMULATION §5).
2. **Sensor naming:** two-tier rule (four volume kinds + channel names) instead of the strict four-kind list (ASSET_PIPELINE §3.5).
3. **Backlot default weather is Calm** (wind 0). Gusty (6 m/s) douses unshielded flames, which is intended: weather matters.
4. **Hover Pad:** rises 0.5 m above where it is placed.
5. **Partner drone redesigned** so it never reads as Buzz (ui_icons); art director to review.
6. **Dynamo** no longer carries the high-voltage pictogram (only Mains and Arc Tower do).
7. **Pedal Rig** budgeted at 15k tris inside the XL class (Bandit + rig ≤ 60k).
