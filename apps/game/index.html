Every action has an overreaction.

Build with the keyboard: 1 to 0 or [ and ] choose a part; arrow keys move the cursor, Enter places; R, T and Y rotate; V selects, E wires, and in the Wire tool [ and ] step through ports; Shift and arrows orbit the camera. Space lets it rip, Backspace stops. Ctrl K opens the command palette. Tab moves into the controls.
