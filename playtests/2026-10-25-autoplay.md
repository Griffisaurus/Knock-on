# Playtest 2026-10-25 (b): THE GRAND KNOCK-ON (autonomous build of every part)

> **Automated session, not a playtest with people.** Stream P5 drove one headless Chrome through the game's own input only: CDP mouse events (clicks, drags, right-drag orbit, middle-drag pan, wheel), key events and `Input.insertText`. It used the lobby, hotbar, command palette, Library, parts tray, radial menu, Inspector, Wire tool and Path tool. `Runtime.evaluate` was used only to **read** state: screen positions of parts and ports, the doc, the camera, DOM rectangles, events and toasts. No part, link or prop was created through an internal API. The machine was designed and tuned first in the headless sim (`packages/sim` `Machine`), then built through the UI from that spec.

## What was built: 370 parts, 135 links, 117 / 117 part types

The stations are laid out in two rows of four, joined at the east end. Row A runs west to east (S1 to S4) and row B runs back east to west (S5 to S8). Every station's parts come from the Library's own demo recipes (`packages/parts-data` demos), with their THUD-on-THE-BUTTON starters replaced by signals from the previous station.

## Notes

### What worked
- **The whole UI build path works at scale.** 370 placements and 1,000+ Inspector edits produced 0 failures. A part-by-part comparison of the doc against the spec found every pose (to 1 mm), rotation, prop, flag and all 135 links correct.
- **The machine runs end to end** in every full-length run without an early poke, with the same hand-off times as the headless sim (to ±0.03 s): pad 1.68 s, lift 7.35 s, PING 7.38 s, critter boom 12.78 s, THE BUTTON 19.06 s, hourglass 27.99 s, RIFT 28.54 s, recycler 34.46 s, plunger 35.13 s, singularity 37.77 s. The browser and the headless `Machine` agree.
- **Future Tech:**
  - RIFT GATES auto-paired, and the pair chips moved both gates to Beam with a clear "Paired with…" line.
  - BUZZ docked by itself, fetched the tagged STITCH and delivered it.
  - STASIS held a BOING and a STITCH in mid-air for 28 s.
  - SINGULARITY swallowed the finale (with the GRAV WELL pulling debris in).
- **Matter:** the hoppers, TANK and GOO VAT poured (133k grains). The WATERWHEEL turned (17°, slowly), the SPONGE squeezed, the SPRINKLE CAN emptied and DUSTY patrolled. The world card's meter read "133k / 4M grains".

### Where the script got stuck (what a player would hit)
- **Radial menu, mouse flow (bug 4).** HOW_TO_PLAY says: hold Q over a part, point, let go. With Keyboard building aids on (the default), Q is taken by the keyboard aids. The menu opens **sticky, at the canvas centre**, not at the cursor, and stays open after Q is released. Only Q-tap + arrows + Enter worked.
- **Cascade Report actions are hidden (bug 6).** At 1920 × 1080 with a 17-link chain, the REPLAY / Share card / Record clip row sits below the panel's fold, just over the parts tray. Clicks there hit the tray. The panel must be scrolled first (it overflows by 65 px).
- **Undo straight after an edit does nothing.** Ctrl+Z within ~0.4 s of placing a part was ignored (the edit was not yet acknowledged by the host). It worked after ~0.9 s. A person may well hit this. **UIFIX (D122):** not reproduced with real input at 0–900 ms after a click, keyboard or drag placement (each undo worked, with the edit still unacknowledged at 0 ms); the one path found that loses an early undo, an undo overtaking an edit still queued by the 150 ops/s window, is fixed. See [BETA_NOTES_UIFIX](../docs/dev/BETA_NOTES_UIFIX.md).
- **Layout constraint (docs).** A Warehouse world is "64 × 64 m", but in the Studio its cove starts curving up 12 m from centre (x beyond ±12 m, z below −8 m). Parts there sit on a ramp: a RIFT GATE lying at x = 13.4 m stopped teleporting because the floor is 18 cm higher there. The machine moved to an Arena (flat ±24 m). The size table could say how much of the footprint is flat.
- **A pad can be pressed again by strays.** A STOMP PAD that toggles MAINS can be pressed again by any stray ball, which turns a whole station off. In the dry run a loose STITCH rolled onto the S1 pad at 4.2 s and switched S2 off. The spec now uses a one-shot latch (FABRICATOR with 1 part → RECYCLER, goal 1). A one-shot option on STOMP PAD, or an "on" signal mode for MAINS, would make chains easier to build.
- **Small UI paper cuts the script worked around:**
  - Typing a part's name in the palette also lists "Parts in scene" rows ("STITCH #123"), and running one of those selects that part and moves the camera.
  - The Inspector body scrolls under the mouse wheel, but a wheel over a slider would change the value.
